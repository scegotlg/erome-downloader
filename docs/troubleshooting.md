# Erome Downloader Troubleshooting Guide
Having trouble finding or downloading a video with Erome Downloader? This guide covers common issues with video URLs, search results, previews, download formats, and network access.

If you need step-by-step instructions, see the [Erome Downloader Usage Guide](usage.md).

## 1. Video URL Cannot Be Processed

**Possible causes:**
- The URL is incomplete or incorrect.
- The link points to a profile or another page type rather than an individual video.
- The source video is unavailable or restricted.
- The source website has changed how its pages are accessed.

**What to try:**
1. Copy the URL directly from the original video page.
2. Check that the page opens normally in your browser.
3. Paste the complete URL into Erome Downloader and try again.
4. If the link still fails, try another supported video URL.

Not every URL can be processed successfully, even when the original page is accessible.

## 2. Keyword Search Returns No Results

**Possible causes:**
- The search terms are too specific or contain a typo.
- No matching content is currently available.
- The source or search service is temporarily unavailable.

**What to try:**
1. Check your spelling.
2. Try a shorter or more general keyword.
3. Use a direct video URL if you already know which video you want.
4. Try again later if the search service appears temporarily unavailable.

Search results may vary depending on the keywords and available source content.

## 3. Video Preview Does Not Load

**Possible causes:**
- The original video is unavailable.
- The source website restricts access to the video.
- Your network connection is unstable.
- The browser cannot load the preview correctly.

**What to try:**
1. Open the original video page and check whether the video plays.
2. Refresh Erome Downloader and try the preview again.
3. Check your internet connection.
4. Try a different supported video URL.

If the original video cannot be accessed, the preview may not work either.

## 4. Download Does Not Start

**Possible causes:**
- The download request failed.
- The source website temporarily restricted access.
- The browser blocked or interrupted the download.
- A network or IP restriction affected the request.

**What to try:**
1. Select the video and format again.
2. Retry the download after checking that the source video is available.
3. Check your browser's download list for a paused, blocked, or failed download.
4. Review your browser's download permissions and available device storage.
5. Try again later if the issue appears temporary.

If the problem persists, try another supported video URL to determine whether the issue affects one video or multiple videos.

## 5. 720p or 1080p Is Not Available

**Why this happens:**

Available download resolutions depend on the source video and the formats that can be retrieved. A video may not have a 1080p version available to the tool, even if you can view it on the original website.

**What to try:**
1. Check all resolution options displayed for the video.
2. Choose the highest available resolution if image quality is your priority.
3. Choose 480p or 720p if a smaller file is more convenient.

Selecting a higher resolution in another player does not guarantee that the same resolution will be available for download.

## 6. MP3 Download Is Missing or Fails

**Possible causes:**
- MP3 output is not available for the selected video.
- The source video cannot be processed.
- The conversion or download request failed.

**What to try:**
1. Check whether MP3 is listed among the available output options.
2. Try processing the video URL again.
3. If MP3 is unavailable, check whether another supported format is offered.

Audio extraction availability may vary by video. Do not assume that every source video can be converted to MP3.

## 7. The Website or Download Is Blocked by Network or IP Restrictions

Some source websites apply access restrictions based on network conditions, IP addresses, or other factors. These restrictions can prevent a video from loading or being processed.

**What to try:**
1. Check whether the original video page is accessible in your browser.
2. Retry later in case the issue is temporary.
3. Test another supported URL to see whether the problem is specific to one source.
4. If the online tool continues to fail, consider whether a separate downloader application supports your use case.

A separate application may behave differently, but it is not guaranteed to resolve network or IP restrictions. Always follow the applicable website terms and local laws.

## 8. Can I Download All Videos from a Username?

No. The online Erome Downloader does not support downloading every video associated with a username in one operation.

To download an individual video, use its supported video URL or find it through keyword search. Process each video separately.

## 9. Downloaded File Cannot Be Found

**What to try:**
1. Open your browser's download list.
2. Check whether the download completed or failed.
3. Use the browser's option to show the file in its folder, if available.
4. Check your device's default Downloads folder or the save location configured in your browser.
5. On a mobile device, also check the browser's download list or the device's file manager.

The exact save location depends on your browser and device settings.

## Still Having Problems?

Open [Erome Downloader](https://usoon.ai/tools/erome-downloader/) and try processing the video again.

For instructions on finding a video, previewing it, and selecting an output format, see the [Usage Guide](usage.md).
