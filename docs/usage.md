# Erome Downloader Usage Guide

This guide explains how to use the [Erome Downloader](https://usoon.ai/tools/erome-downloader/) to find, preview, and download individual videos. You can use a video URL or search by keyword, preview available content, and choose an available video resolution or audio format.

## 1. Open Erome Downloader

Visit the [Erome Downloader online tool](https://usoon.ai/tools/erome-downloader/) in your desktop or mobile browser.

No account is required to use the online tool.

## 2. Find a Video

You can find a video in two ways, depending on the options available in the tool.

### Option A: Use a Video URL

1. Open the page containing the video you want to download.
2. Copy the video URL from your browser's address bar.
3. Return to Erome Downloader and paste the URL into the input field.
4. Start the search or parsing process.

Make sure you copy the URL of the video page rather than an unrelated profile or website URL. The tool can only process links supported by the source website.

### Option B: Search by Keyword

1. Enter a relevant keyword in the search field.
2. Start the search.
3. Review the available results.
4. Select a video to preview before downloading.

Keyword search is useful when you do not have a direct video URL. Available results depend on the search terms and source availability.

## 3. Preview the Video

When a video is available, preview it before downloading.

Checking the preview helps you confirm that you have selected the intended video. Review the available title, thumbnail, or other displayed details when provided.

If the preview does not load, try opening the original video page to check whether the content is still available. You can also try a different supported URL.

## 4. Choose a Format and Resolution

Erome Downloader may offer different download options depending on the source video.

| Option | Best for |
|---|---|
| MP4 480p | Smaller files and situations where lower resolution is sufficient |
| MP4 720p | A balance between image quality and file size |
| MP4 1080p | Higher-resolution playback when available |
| MP3 | Saving the audio without the video |

Not every video is available in every resolution or format. The options displayed by the tool determine what you can download for that particular video.

A higher resolution generally produces a larger file. If storage space or download speed is a concern, consider choosing a lower resolution.

## 5. Download the Video

After selecting a video and an available format:

1. Choose the preferred resolution or MP3 option.
2. Click the corresponding download control.
3. Follow any on-screen instructions to start the download.
4. Check your browser's download list to confirm that the file has finished downloading.

On a desktop computer, downloaded files usually appear in your browser's configured download folder. On a mobile device, the save location depends on your browser and device settings.

If the download does not start, check the [Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting.md).

## 6. Can You Download All Videos from a Username?

The online tool does not support entering a username to download every video associated with that account.

To download a specific video, process its supported video URL individually. Repeat these steps for each video you want to save.

## 7. Tips for Better Results

- **Use the direct video page URL.** Profile links and other page types may not be supported.
- **Preview before downloading.** Confirm that the selected result matches the video you want.
- **Check the available formats.** Resolution and audio options can vary between videos.
- **Check source availability.** Removed, private, restricted, or otherwise inaccessible content may not be downloadable.
- **Consider network restrictions.** Some websites may restrict access based on network or IP address. Results can vary, and changing software does not guarantee that these restrictions will be resolved.
- **Try another supported link.** If one URL fails, confirm that the original video is accessible and try again.

## 8. Privacy and Responsible Use

The online tool does not require an account, displays no advertisements, and does not keep a download history. According to the tool's stated privacy practices, downloaded videos are not stored on its server.

These statements do not mean that the website or its hosting providers process no technical data. Review the website's privacy information for further details.

Only download content you are authorized to save, and respect the rights and terms that apply to the original content.
