## ⚠️Limitations

Please keep the following limitations in mind:

- **No username-based bulk downloads:** You cannot enter a username to download every video uploaded by that account in one operation. Each video must be processed individually.
- **Not every link is guaranteed to work:** Some videos may be unavailable because of source website restrictions, unsupported links, or changes to the source page.
- **Resolution options may vary:** A particular video may not offer every listed resolution.
- **Network and IP restrictions may affect access:** If a source website blocks access from your network or IP address, the online tool may be unable to retrieve the video.
